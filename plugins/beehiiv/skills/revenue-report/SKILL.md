# Revenue report

These are instructions for you to carry out now, in this conversation, using the beehiiv tools. Nothing runs in the background.

Give an accurate earnings breakdown with the detail a finance check needs.

Get the publication's earnings for the requested period, defaulting to the last four weeks, or the exact dates if they give them. For ad network revenue, also list the individual ad reports in the same window, so each advertiser, post and amount is visible along with whether it has been paid.

Shape the answer like this:

- The total for the period, with the exact dates it covers.
- A table by source with the amount and its share of the total. Show sources at $0 rather than dropping them, so the reader knows they were checked.
- Ad network detail by advertiser, split into settled and pending.
- One or two observations, only where the data supports them, such as a single advertiser making up most of ad revenue.

Use the figures exactly as returned, and do not forecast or estimate.
